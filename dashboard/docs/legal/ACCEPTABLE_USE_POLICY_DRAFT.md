# Acceptable Use Policy — Draft for Legal Review

**Not legal advice. Requires qualified legal review before launch.**

Users must not use the service for unauthorized account access, credential
stuffing/theft, phishing, malware, fraud, impersonation, access-control bypass,
security evasion, spam, harassment, illegal surveillance, abusive or prohibited
scraping, attacks on private/internal networks, purchases of controlled or
prohibited goods, or other harmful/unlawful automation. Users must not evade
target-site security or the platform's payment, upload/download, network,
domain, rate-limit or execution controls.

Phase 11/20 controls can block known unsafe destinations/actions, excessive
requests and abusive admission patterns, but cannot detect every misuse.
